Student Notes privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Student Notes is a note-taking app for students. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how you can delete it. The short version: your notes are yours, there are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Using the app without an account

If you choose “Use on this phone only”, everything you create stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Using the app with Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Firebase (a service run by Google) to keep your notes in sync between your devices.

WhatWhere it is storedWhy
Your Google account name, email address and a Firebase user IDFirebase AuthenticationTo know which notes belong to you
Your typed notes, subjects, tags, and the names and details of files you importCloud Firestore, in a space only your account can readTo sync and restore your notes
Photos and PDFs you import (only if you connect Google Drive)A “Student Notes” folder in your own Google DriveTo back up your files

Google Drive access uses the narrowest permission Google offers (drive.file): the app can see only the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

The original photos and PDFs in your gallery or Downloads are never changed or uploaded; the app works with its own copy.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sync happens only when you tap “Sync now”.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with anyone. Google processes it only to run Firebase and Drive for the app.
  • We don’t read your notes.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Storage & sync and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it, removes it from your phone, and can also move the “Student Notes” folder in your Drive to the trash.
  • Without the app: email moki.officiial@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject “Delete my Student Notes account”. We delete the account and its synced data within 30 days and reply when it’s done.
  • Files in your Google Drive belong to you; you can also delete the “Student Notes” folder there at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
